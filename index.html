Bio

Ayesh is Co-Founder & CTO at CVision, building production AI at the intersection of efficient edge computing, industrial computer vision, and applied ML. His work spans low-latency inference on embedded hardware, high-precision factory CV, LLM alignment and evaluation pipelines, and AI-driven operational intelligence—serving US and EU clients across retail, logistics, and manufacturing. He leads technical strategy, systems architecture, and end-to-end delivery from prototype to deployed product.
